# Privacy Policy

## SHAHEEN OS

SHAHEEN OS is designed with privacy and user control in mind.

The exact data processed by a deployment depends on the enabled
features, integrations, AI providers, databases and external services.

### Self-Hosted Deployments

For self-hosted installations, the operator is responsible for:

- infrastructure
- database configuration
- authentication
- logs
- backups
- external integrations
- AI provider configuration
- data retention

### AI Providers

If external AI providers are configured, data may be transmitted
to those providers according to the provider's configuration,
terms and privacy policy.

### User Responsibility

Operators should configure:

- data retention policies
- access controls
- encryption
- logging policies
- backup policies
- provider permissions
