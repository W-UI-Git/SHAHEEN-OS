## Gradient
#24D6E8 → #2563EB → #6366F1 → #EC4899

## Recommended typography
Latin: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans
Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic / Noto Sans Arabic
Use a bold weight for the wordmark and 500–700 for UI headings.

## Logo rules
- Keep clear space around the symbol equal to at least 20% of its width.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or recolor the logo outside the approved palette.
- Prefer the dark logo on dark surfaces and the light wordmark on light surfaces.
- Minimum symbol size: 24 px digital, 8 mm print.
- Minimum wordmark width: 120 px digital.
